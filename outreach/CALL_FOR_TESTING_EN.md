# Open call: challenge CI-001 on one system

Can a governance tool distinguish a path that has disappeared from evidence that has merely not been obtained?

CI-001 examines three structural conditions in a scoped system: correction (C), exit or refusal (E), and current recovery conditions (R). Rebuildability is assessed separately. V1.5-RC2 is now open for testing. Endorsement is not requested; reproducible failure is.

Choose any route:

- run the deterministic AI validation suite;
- give the answer-free hypothetical scenario to one or more AI systems and compare where their results diverge;
- choose a real system you know and challenge one clause that is unusable or has two plausible readings.

One problem is enough. Identify the exact wording, assessment unit, evidence or hypothetical label, and a workable alternative. AI results are labelled internal validation; human participation is voluntary and is not a release gate. Testing does not imply endorsement or certification.
