## 2. 正文修订对照

### T01｜§1.3 自评估中的“内部责任人”改写

**旧文定位：** 自评估段要求指定内部责任人；英文为“name an internal owner”。该要求对没有运营组织的受影响者容易成为虚构字段。

**动作：** 将自评估段关于披露和指定责任的句子替换为下文，其余保留。

**中文候选：**

应披露自评估性质、利益关系和证据缺口，明确本记录的维护者、既有权限内的整改责任及复审日期。由开发方或运营方采用时，应指定内部责任人；由不具运营权限的受影响者或其他单方使用者采用时，应记录可提出的请求、拟联系的承接方，以及尚未落实的整改或补证责任，不得把拟请求对象写成已经承担义务的一方。四个维度的内部核查不冒充独立复核。

**English candidate:**

Disclose the self-assessment status, interests, and evidence gaps. Identify the record keeper, responsibility for corrective action within existing authority, and the next review date. A developer or operator adopting the instrument must name an internal owner. An affected party or other unilateral adopter without operational authority must record the requests they can make, the proposed recipient or support provider, and any corrective or evidence-collection responsibilities that remain unassigned. A proposed recipient must not be presented as already obliged to respond. Internal checks across four dimensions must not be represented as independent review.

### T02｜§1.3 末尾：采用模式与填写身份分开

**旧文定位：** 现有段落允许其他单方使用者，但没有受影响者自填示例。

**动作：** 新增一段。

**中文候选：**

采用模式与填写者身份分别记录。受影响主体本人、候选主体的记录提供者或其代表，无需具有系统运营权限，即可提交自述、路径证据和初步判断；代表关系及授权不足应明示。单方采用时，按自评估标注，并注明与被审查机制及控制关系的位置、实际权限、利益关系和未完成的独立复核。提交证据与提出判断不等于控制独立最终裁决；参与权不自动构成最终表决资格。不得仅以提交者为受影响者降低其证据的证明力，也不得仅因其为当事者免除核验。转交者与原记录提供者应分别署明，不冒充亲历观察。

**English candidate:**

Record adoption mode separately from the contributor’s role. An affected party, a person or system providing records concerning a candidate subject, or a representative may submit self-reports, path evidence, and preliminary judgments without operational authority. Disclose the basis and any limits of representation. Label unilateral use as self-assessment and identify the contributor’s position in the mechanism and control relationship, actual authority, interests, and incomplete independent review. Submitting evidence or proposing a judgment is not control of an independent final determination; a right to participate does not automatically confer a final vote. Evidence must not be given less weight solely because its provider is affected, nor accepted without verification solely for that reason. Identify a forwarding party separately from the original record provider; forwarding is not firsthand observation.

### T03｜§2.1 E末尾：拒绝、退出与实例结束

**旧文定位：** 当前E把拒绝操作、终止关系与替代安排并列；成本段禁止以丧失基本存续条件为前提。

**动作：** 新增一段；成本与承接规则保留。

**中文候选：**

应注明E所指的具体操作、控制关系与评估层级。拒绝单次操作、退出控制关系和终止运行实例，不自动等价；拒绝表达是否产生实际阻断效果、是否保留必要承接条件，须分别取证。任务结束与主体存续中止的关系，不得仅凭系统名称、会话形式或语言自述推定。涉及不同关键能力或层级时，分别建立评估单元，各自记录同一范围的C、E、R，不拼接、不抵消。对其他任务的拒绝不能抵充本次被审查能力的退出或拒绝路径。

**English candidate:**

Specify the operation, control relationship, and assessment level to which E refers. Refusing an individual operation, leaving a control relationship, and ending a running instance are not automatically equivalent. Establish separately whether an expression of refusal actually blocks the operation and whether necessary continuity arrangements remain available. Do not infer that task completion ends the subject’s continued existence from a system name, conversational format, or verbal self-report alone. Use separate assessment units for different critical capabilities or levels, each with C, E, and R covering the same scope. Do not combine states across units or use effectiveness in one to cancel failure in another. Refusal of unrelated tasks does not establish an exit or refusal path for the capability under assessment.

### T04｜§2.2末尾：一条失败路径与整项失效

**旧文定位：** 无法取得证据应记未知；当前没有直接说明多个相关路径如何形成单项取值。

**动作：** 新增填写说明。

**中文候选：**

一条路径失败不单独证明整项条件失效。针对已明确的评估范围，存在至少一条满足本项全部要求且经核验有效的路径，方可支持该项记为1；有充分证据支持相关可行路径均失效，方可记为0。已证实某路径失败而其他相关路径尚未查明时，保留?并记录已确认问题。不得以没有任何依据的想象路径无限阻止作出结论；路径范围、排查依据和证据局限须可复核。

**English candidate:**

Failure of one path does not by itself establish failure of the entire condition. Within the defined assessment scope, a value of 1 requires at least one verified effective path meeting all requirements of that condition; a value of 0 requires sufficient evidence that the relevant feasible paths are ineffective. Where one path is verified as failed but other relevant paths remain unresolved, retain ? and record the verified problem. Unsupported imagined paths must not indefinitely prevent a finding. Document the path scope, the basis for examining or excluding paths, and evidentiary limitations.

### T05｜§2.4“证据不足”整段替换

**旧文定位：** “保留未知并指定补证责任与期限”；英文“assign evidence work and a deadline”。

**中文候选：**

证据不足：尚不能确认重建可行，也不足以证明不可重建。保留未知，不得升级为不可逆确认。未知原因应记录为尚未检索、证据相互矛盾、无法安全验证、缺乏取证位置或其他注明原因。分别记录证据持有方及其认定依据、请求提出者、协调者、核验者和各自权限；角色未落实时如实注明。持有方可以是被评估方，但其提供材料不等于独立核验通过。无调查权限的填写者记录请求对象和请求状态，不虚构强制义务。保留有依据的关键窗口、请求期限和下一复核时点；无法确定时间时说明原因。未提供证据是独立的程序事实，不单独证明路径失效或不可逆；是否违反既有响应义务，另列义务依据与证据。

**English candidate:**

Insufficient evidence: rebuilding has not been confirmed feasible, but non-rebuildability has not been established either. Retain the unknown and do not upgrade it to confirmed irreversibility. Record the reason: not yet investigated, conflicting evidence, inability to verify safely, lack of evidence access, or another stated reason. Separately identify the evidence holder and the basis for identifying them, the requester, the coordinator, the verifier, and each role’s authority; disclose unassigned roles. The evaluated party may hold evidence, but supplying it does not establish successful independent verification. A contributor without investigative authority records requests and their status without inventing compulsory duties. Retain a justified critical window, request deadline, and next review time; explain any timing that cannot be determined. Non-provision of evidence is a separate procedural fact, not by itself proof of path failure or irreversibility. Any alleged breach of an existing response duty requires a separately stated basis and evidence.

## 4. 配套填写指引候选（forms/README）

### F01｜替换首次用时段并新增角色说明

**中文可用文本：**

先选一个主体、一项关键能力、一个范围与时间窗口；无需运营权限即可开始记录。填写者本人可以是受影响者。A1分别注明采用模式、与系统的关系、承担职能、实际权限和利益关系；转交他人材料时注明原记录者。自述、初步判断和独立最终裁决是不同职能。

可预留约一小时整理已有证据并做首次填写。这是规划估计，不是经验证的完成时限；完整取证、试验、协调与独立复核另计。不必一次填完，无法支持的判断保留未知。较长角色说明、证据和补证请求另附编号，表单作为索引。

**English ready text:**

Begin with one party, one critical capability, one scope and one time window. Operational authority is not required to start a record, and an affected party may complete the form. In A1, distinguish adoption mode, relationship to the system, function, actual authority and interests. Identify the original record provider when forwarding material. Self-report, preliminary judgment and independent final determination are different functions.

Allow roughly an hour to organize existing evidence and make an initial entry. This is a planning estimate, not a validated completion time; evidence collection, testing, coordination and independent review take additional time. Partial completion is welcome. Keep unsupported findings unknown. Use attachment IDs for extended role descriptions, evidence and evidence requests; the form is their index.

### F02｜配套角色与补证记录字段

角色记录：记录编号；人员或系统标识；候选主体资格如适用；与被审查机制和控制关系的位置；承担职能；既有权限；利益关系；代表/转交依据；独立复核状态。

Role record: record ID; person or system identifier; candidate status if applicable; position in the mechanism and control relationship; function; existing authority; interests; representation/forwarding basis; independent-review status.

补证记录：未知项及对应评估单元；未知原因；证据持有方及依据；请求方、协调方、核验方；响应义务依据或未确认；请求日、请求期限、关键窗口及依据；响应/未响应/未请求/无法联系等状态；已有材料与替代取证；下一复核日期；有权处置方或尚未落实。

Evidence-request record: unknown item and assessment unit; reason unknown; evidence holder and basis; requester, coordinator, verifier; response-duty basis or unconfirmed; request date, requested deadline, critical window and basis; response/no response/not requested/unreachable status; material received and alternative evidence; next review; authorized responder or unassigned.

### F03｜双语反例与示例发布定位

C反例：可提交工单，但没有可执行的响应和纠正安排，不能仅凭入口记1。其他相关路径未查明时记?；不是见到一条失败路径就记0。

C counterexample: a ticket can be submitted, but no executable response and correction arrangement is established. The entry point alone does not justify 1. Retain ? when other relevant paths remain unresolved; one failed path does not justify 0.

E反例：删除争议服务账户同时切断唯一必要供给，且没有已核实承接；该按钮不能证明受保护的退出。其他拒绝或替代路径仍须查证。

E counterexample: deleting the disputed service account also removes the only essential supply, with no verified continuity arrangement. The button does not establish protected exit. Other refusal or alternative paths still require examination.
