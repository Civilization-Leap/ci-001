# CI-001 · Irreversible Closure Criterion

**V1.5-RC2 · Unpublished review candidate · F1.1-RC1**

Before the last effective path disappears.

Check one party’s critical capability for correction C, exit or refusal E, and current recovery R. Record 1 for verified effectiveness, 0 for verified failure, and ? for insufficient evidence. R = 0 is not irreversibility; 0/0/0 still requires a separate Section 2.4 finding.

**See how it is filled:** [completed one-page form](examples/HYP-001_Filled_EN.docx) → [full hypothetical evidence](examples/HYP-001_EN.md) → [completion guide](forms/Guide_EN.md).

The manual and guide are V1.5-RC2. Blank and completed one-page forms retain unchanged F1.1-RC1 bytes and historical labels, compatible with the same 16 fields. Record V1.5-RC2 in A1 for a new assessment; the completed specimen is hypothetical.

**Start a record:** [English one-page Word form](forms/CI-001_V1.5-RC1_One-page_Audit_Form_EN.docx) · [中文一页Word表](forms/CI-001_V1.5-RC1_一页可填审计表_中文.docx) · [all formats and completion guide](forms/README.md). Choose one party, one critical capability, one scope and one time window. Developers, laboratories, operators, reviewers and affected parties themselves may use the tool. Record your role, interests and actual authority in A1.

**How to begin:** Organize available evidence first; partial records and unknowns are welcome. About an hour is only an initial planning estimate; evidence collection, testing and independent review take additional time. Record task refusal and relationship exit separately when they belong to different assessment units. If evidence is held outside the system, record its holder, request status and next review; lack of your own access does not establish that the evidence or recovery conditions do not exist.

**Effect of findings:** Self-assessment may report evidence and preliminary judgments. It confers neither enforcement authority over third parties nor independent adjudication or certification.
