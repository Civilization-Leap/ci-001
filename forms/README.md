# Start with one page / 从一页开始

Use the Chinese or English Word form; each has 16 editable text controls covering
the two Appendix A tables. The interactive PDFs have the same one-page layout and 16 text fields; Chinese and English test values survived filling, saving and reopening in the validation toolchain.
Long evidence belongs in attachments with IDs; the form is an index to evidence,
not a substitute for the full record. Filling it may expand the Word page if a
long answer is entered; preserve the one-page summary by moving detail to evidence.

Suggested first pass, not a validated completion-time claim: 10 minutes for scope,
25 for C/E/R, 15 for rebuilding and authority, 10 for actions, unknowns and review.
Existing evidence can be attached. Do not manufacture evidence to meet an hour.

The five candidate-party clues, when relevant, are continuity as a whole;
integration of perception and memory; expression of boundaries and wishes;
capacity to be harmed as a whole; and capacity to carry meaning. Record limits and
unknowns; these are not a validated consciousness test or an exhaustive checklist.
