# CI-001 identity and conformity / 名称与一致性

The copyright license and a claim about what a tool implements are separate.
CC BY 4.0 permits adapted versions, including versions that remove safeguards,
subject to its attribution and change-notice terms. This document does not revoke
that permission and does not assert a registered trademark or certification scheme.

Within this project's version descriptions, **“CI-001 V1.4”** identifies the
specified criterion. Use **“CI-001 V1.4-conformant adaptation”** for an adaptation
only if it preserves the substantive requirements below and the applicable C/E/R
definitions, scope and decision sequence. Retaining section numbers or headings
while nullifying their effect does not meet the description.

| Required safeguard | What must remain effective |
| --- | --- |
| §2.4 Evidence and rebuilding | Current R and irreversibility are separate; four rebuilding findings, explicit assumptions/windows, unknowns and evidence duties remain. |
| §3.3 Control of interventions | Assess interventions themselves; preserve essential services, evidence and succession; limit intervention to what its evidence supports. |
| §4.1 False findings | Withdraw errors and restrictions based only on them; repair recoverable effects, record correction and preserve appeal. |
| §4.2 Misses and unknowns | Assign evidence owners and deadlines, reopen on supported triggers, and bound the intervention's scope, duration, appeal and termination. |

For a different design, use a different title and say **“adapted from CI-001 V1.4;
modified §§…”**. Accurate attribution, discussion, criticism, and descriptive
reference to CI-001 remain permitted. Do not suggest author endorsement.

中文：CC BY允许删改内容；本文只界定本项目怎样准确描述“一致”。改编者自由复用、
翻译或内嵌，保留上述四节实质及适用C／E／R规则时，可称“与CI-001 V1.4一致的改编”。
删去约束后的作品另命名，并注明“改编自CI-001 V1.4，已修改……”。仅保留节号而削弱
约束不算一致。正常署名、批评、讨论和说明来源不受这一表述规则阻碍。

Minimum preservation is necessary, not by itself proof of full conformity or
correct application. No independent certification is supplied by this repository.
