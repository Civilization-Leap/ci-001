# TRIAL-EXT-01 External Trial Protocol and Feedback Record

**Version 0.1 · CI-001 V1.5-RC2 · 2026-09-09 · Zijun Fu**

Use this protocol to test whether CI-001 can be applied consistently without the author present. It tests usability and interpretation. It does not request endorsement and does not produce certification.

## Choose one route

**Route A — Full trial.** Apply the 16 fields to one affected party, one critical capability, one system boundary and one time window. A partial record is acceptable when evidence is unavailable.

**Route B — Clause challenge.** Select one clause that cannot be applied, produces two defensible readings, or assigns evidence or authority to the wrong party. Record the system, evidence and a workable alternative.

## Trial rules

1. State your role and relationship to the system. Mark self-assessment and independent review accurately.
2. Separate observed evidence, inference and unknowns. Lack of evidence remains `?`; it does not become `0` without evidence that a path failed.
3. Record C, E and R as `1`, `0` or `?`. R concerns current recovery. Assess rebuilding separately under §2.4.
4. State the closing event and the time needed for initiation, coordination and execution. Do not invent a deadline to force a finding.
5. If evidence is held elsewhere, record the holder, request status and response. Nonresponse is a procedural fact, not automatic proof of failure.
6. Record actual authority. A self-assessor may recommend preservation or pause activity they control, but does not acquire power over third parties.
7. Artificial or nonhuman affected parties may be provisionally included under §1.2. Self-report alone does not establish consciousness or status.
8. Do not read the maintainer’s answer for a shared test case until after recording your own values.

## Suggested procedure

1. Fix the assessment unit and start time.
2. List evidence IDs and access limits.
3. Record C/E/R and reasons.
4. Make the separate §2.4 rebuilding finding.
5. Complete the feedback record below.
6. Submit through the external-trial issue template or email the completed record.

## What the project will measure

The project will record completion time, the proportion of `?`, disagreements over the same evidence, clauses with repeated ambiguity, and whether evidence failure arises from wording, permissions or absent institutions. Individual records will not be described as adoption, endorsement or independent review beyond what the tester explicitly confirms.
